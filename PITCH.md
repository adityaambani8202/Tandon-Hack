# Pitch + demo script

## 60-second pitch (CEO records)
"Every year millions of people leave the hospital with a stack of papers, while tired, medicated and stressed. Elderly patients, who have the most medications and appointments, get the least out of it. Missed doses and ignored warning signs send people back to the hospital.

Recovery Reminder fixes that. Snap a photo of your discharge papers. We pull out every medication, appointment, diet rule and warning sign, show you exactly which line of the paper each came from, and you confirm it. Then you get a plain-language daily plan with reminders. Family can follow along, and doctors see who needs attention.

We never give medical advice; we only repeat what your care team already said. Insurers and hospitals pay because every avoided readmission saves thousands. Medisafe reminds you about pills and MyChart stores records. We go from paperwork to a full recovery plan."

## 3-minute live demo (no slides)
1. Open the live URL on a phone. Say: "A patient just got discharged."
2. Upload a discharge sheet (or tap sample). Show the confirm screen: "Every item shows the exact quote from the paper. Nothing starts until a human confirms."
3. Confirm. Today view: tap Taken on one dose, Skipped on another. Tap the speaker icon to hear the reminder.
4. Check-in tab: tick fever, submit. Red alert appears: "your own papers say to call the doctor."
5. Ask tab: "Can I lift groceries?" (answers from the papers). Then "Should I double my dose?" It refuses and says ask your care team.
6. Appointments: download the calendar file. Doctor tab: 7-day adherence and PDF export.
7. Close: "Be honest about the roadmap: push/SMS reminders, caregiver accounts, Fitbit, HIPAA hosting."

## If something breaks
Use "Try with sample papers" (works with no internet/API). Pre-open the URL 5 minutes early so the free server is awake.
